# 🚗 Car Market Trends Analysis with CarDekho Data

A Python-based data analysis project that explores **CarDekho car market data** to identify trends in car prices, brands, fuel types, transmission, ownership, and other key vehicle characteristics.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes used-car market data from CarDekho to understand pricing patterns and customer preferences.

The analysis focuses on identifying factors that influence **car prices, demand, and market trends**.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python**
* **Pandas**
* **NumPy**
* **Matplotlib**
* **Seaborn**
* **Jupyter Notebook**
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 🚘 Brand Analysis

Compared car brands based on availability, pricing, and market presence.

### 💰 Price Analysis

Analyzed car prices and identified pricing patterns across different vehicle categories.

### ⛽ Fuel Type Analysis

Compared cars based on fuel types such as Petrol, Diesel, CNG, and other available categories.

### ⚙️ Transmission Analysis

Analyzed the distribution and pricing of Manual and Automatic vehicles.

### 👤 Ownership Analysis

Examined the impact of previous ownership on car prices.

### 📅 Year-wise Analysis

Studied how car manufacturing year affects pricing and market trends.

### 📊 Correlation Analysis

Explored relationships between important numerical variables such as price, year, mileage, and other available features.
